Community Guidelines & Safety

Effective · Version 2026-11-15

Be kind. Keep it safe. Keep it legal. These rules are part of the Terms of Service. Roblink is for people 13 and over.

The rules

  1. Treat people with respect. No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, or pile-ons. No hate or discrimination based on who someone is.
  2. Protect young people. Never send sexual content or messages to anyone under 18, ask minors for pictures or personal information, or try to move a minor to another app or to meet in person. Zero tolerance – permanent ban and report to the authorities.
  3. No sexual or graphic content. No porn, nudity, sexual roleplay, or gore.
  4. No dangerous content. Nothing that encourages suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, violence, terrorism, drugs or other crime.
  5. Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal details (address, phone, school, real name, photos) or private messages without their permission. Don't record voice chats without everyone's consent.
  6. Be honest. No impersonation, scams, phishing, fake giveaways, or malware. Roblink staff will never ask for your password.
  7. No spam or abuse of the service. No mass-messaging, raids, bot abuse, hacking or ban evasion.
  8. Follow the law and respect other people's copyright.
  9. Follow server rules as well as these. Server owners and moderators can set extra rules for their communities.

What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, send a warning, timeout or suspend an account (for a set time or permanently), remove a server from Discovery, or delete it. Serious harm (for example anything involving child safety, threats of violence or illegal content) can mean an immediate permanent ban and a report to the police or child-protection organisations.

How to report something

  • Report a message: right-click (or long-press) the message → 🚩 Report.
  • Report a person: open their profile or right-click them → 🚩 Report User.
  • Report a server: open the server menu → 🚩 Report Server.
  • Need help or want to appeal? Open 🛟 Support to start a ticket and chat with our team. Suspended accounts can still use Support to appeal.
  • Block anyone who bothers you, and leave any server or voice channel you're not comfortable in.
In immediate danger? Contact your local emergency services (999 UK · 112 EU · 911 US). Roblink staff cannot respond instantly. If you are struggling, you can talk to someone: in the UK call Samaritans on 116 123, or text SHOUT to 85258; elsewhere see findahelpline.com.

Staying safe online (especially if you're under 18)

  • Don't share your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords or photos of yourself with people you don't know in real life.
  • People online aren't always who they say they are. Be wary of anyone who asks you to keep secrets, move to another app, or send pictures.
  • If something makes you uncomfortable: stop, block, report, and tell a trusted adult.
  • Never click suspicious links or download files from strangers.

For parents and guardians

Roblink is a social chat service where people can talk to others, including people they don't know. Please talk to your child about staying safe, check their privacy settings (Settings → Privacy) and tell them to come to you if something upsets them. You can contact us at to ask questions, report concerns, or request deletion of your child's account.
