Privacy Policy

Effective · Version 2026-11-15

The short version: we collect what we need to run your account and chat (email, username, messages, uploads and basic technical data). We do not sell your data and do not show ads or use tracking cookies. Roblink is for people aged 13 and over. You can ask us to access, fix or delete your data at .

1. Who is responsible for your data

("we", "us") is the "controller" of the personal data described here. Contact: .

2. Children

Roblink is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. At sign-up we ask for your date of birth to check your age; we do not store the date itself – only whether you are 13–17 or 18+, and when you accepted our Terms. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian has read this policy and the Terms. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us at and we will delete it. Parents and guardians can contact us to ask what data we hold or to have it deleted.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your profile, and content you post, is visible to the people in the servers and DMs where you post it. Anyone can see a public server's content if they join it.
  • Roblink staff: limited staff may access account, safety and message data where needed to run the service, review reports, investigate abuse or comply with the law. Staff actions are logged.
  • Our service providers (processors): Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and real-time messaging – hosted in ); GitHub Pages (hosts the website, may log visitor IP addresses); jsDelivr (delivers a code library); Google Fonts (delivers a font; your IP address is sent to Google when it loads); Google STUN servers (help set up voice connections); and our email provider for sign-in and password emails.
  • Authorities: we may disclose data to the police, regulators or courts where legally required, or where we reasonably believe it is needed to protect someone from serious harm or to report child sexual abuse material.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK/EEA (for example in the United States). Where they do, we rely on safeguards such as UK/EU adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, as provided by those providers.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account (or we delete it for inactivity or breach).
  • Messages: until you or a server moderator delete them, the server is deleted, or your account is deleted. Deleted data may remain in provider backups for a short period.
  • Reports, tickets, warnings and staff logs: normally up to 12 months after they are closed (longer if needed to protect people, deal with repeat abuse or meet a legal duty).
  • Suspended/banned accounts: we may keep minimal data (such as email and reason) so banned people cannot simply return.

7. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to some uses; and move it to another service. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. To use a right, open a support ticket (category "Account") or email . We may need to check that it is you. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority – in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Roblink stores a small amount of data in your browser (local storage) that is strictly necessary for it to work: your sign-in session, your settings, your server layout and – if you use account switching – the sign-in details of accounts you saved on that device. Clear it by logging out, removing saved accounts or clearing your browser data.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules in the database. You can add two-factor authentication to your account. No system is completely secure, and direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above (and tell you in the app if the changes are important).
